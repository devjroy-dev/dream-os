# TDW · STRATEGY NOTE — BEYOND THE CRM
**Authored by CE-38 from the founder's own framing · 2026-08-29 · founder-approved for the tree · status: DIRECTION OF RECORD. Future charters cite this note rather than re-deriving it.**

## §1 · WHAT THE PRODUCT IS
A CRM sells organization — the vendor pays to be tidier. TDW sells **outcomes**: enquiries answered in her name (Victor), a public presence she never has to touch (GBP, `/v/`, own domain, SEO), proof documents, review capture, her own number answered by TDW's machinery. The honest name is **the vendor's back office and front door, run for her**. The books-and-calendar layer (Khata, calendar, contracts, TDS) is the *retention* layer, not the product. This was not drift: every founder walk rejected surfaces for "this doesn't get her anything," never for "the data isn't organized." The product followed the founder's thumb.

The **zero-commission subscription** is what makes it coherent: she pays for capability, never for access to demand. TDW never becomes the middleman she resents. That promise is the moat and is not for sale.

## §2 · THE MARKETPLACE — SEQUENCING, NOT MONETIZATION
A marketplace monetizes demand; today the estate has supply-side machinery and a demand engine still being built. Charging vendors for leads before leads exist is the JustDial failure — distrusted on both sides. The ruling of record:
- The marketplace (Discover, couple concierge) stays **free and neutral** as the demand magnet.
- The money stays on the **vendor subscription**.
- The marketplace's job is to make the paid tools undeniable: every Discover card is a live demonstration of a TDW-run presence; every enquiry through Victor is the demo.
- Later levers that do not poison trust: placement **quality** (better storefronts rank naturally — which sells storefront services, never payola), booking-adjacent services (§3), couple-side monetization (planning tools, curated bundles). **Never per-lead tolls, never a cut of her booking.**
