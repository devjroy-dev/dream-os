This joins the two parts you picked. Home is Option C's first screen: what needs her today, then six rooms she pins. It takes the place of Today. Rooms is Option B's shelves, with Business Solutions and Storefront at the very top, where the eye lands first.

TDW chat is left exactly as the app has it now: the Ask TDW bar sits just above the tabs on every screen, with the same words and the same send arrow, and it opens the same chat. Nothing about how she talks to TDW changes.

Rooms keeps today's order of tabs (Rooms on the left, the day's screen on the right), so a vendor who has used the app does not have to relearn where her thumb goes.

Below the top two, three shelves hold everything else: Couples, Money and Studio, never more than six rows each. Inside Business Solutions the eleven rows are sorted into Get found, Get booked, Get paid and Work together.

Storefront and Your website & SEO stay separate rooms here, as you have them today. Contracts appears once, inside Business Solutions under Get paid, and Collab is Hire, collab & barter under Work together. Payment reminders lives only in Business Solutions, under Get paid.

Today: 2 tabs, 20 room tiles, and 11 more rows inside Business Solutions. Here: the same 2 tabs and the same chat bar; Home with 6 pins; Rooms with 2 rooms on top and 3 shelves of at most 6 rows.
