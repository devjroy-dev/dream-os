## 1 · §1 OPT-IN — F-41.126 IS ANSWERED, AND NOT IN THE ESTATE'S FAVOUR

> *You may only contact people on WhatsApp if: **(a) they have given you their mobile phone
> number; and (b) you have received opt-in permission** from the recipient confirming that they
> wish to receive subsequent messages or calls from you.*

**Two limbs, conjunctive.** An outsider vendor hand-picked from Instagram satisfies neither: she
did not give TDW her number, and she gave no permission. **The chair's reading is confirmed at
source, and v2's Utility category is irrelevant to it** — the category governs pricing and
throttling; §1 governs whether the message may be sent at all.

### One refinement R-41.122 needs, and it is limb (a)

R-41.122 cures limb (b): ask in the Instagram DM, record the answer on the `prospects` row, and
the arm refuses without it. **That is right and it is the whole cure for (b).**

**But (a) says she must have GIVEN TDW the number.** If the number was read off her public
Instagram bio and the DM only asks *"may we send you the enquiry on WhatsApp at this number?"*,
then TDW obtained the number and she merely consented to its use. **A strict reading of (a) is
not satisfied.**

**The cheap fix is one word in the DM:** ask her to **give** the number rather than confirm one
TDW already holds — *"…may we send you the enquiry on WhatsApp? Reply with the number to use."*
Her reply then supplies the number and the permission in the same message, and **the recorded
opt-in on the row should store her words verbatim**, because the words are what evidence limb
(a) as well as (b).

**Reported for the chair; the queue's outsider field should capture the reply, not a checkbox.**

**§1 also requires** — mandatory, not best practice — *"Maintain a WhatsApp Business profile with
customer support contact information and one or more of the following: email address, website
address, and/or telephone number."*

## 2 · §2 AUTOMATION NEEDS A VISIBLE ESCALATION PATH — F-41.127, derived

> *You may use automation when responding during the 24-hour window, but **must also have
> available prompt, clear, and direct escalation paths**: In-Chat Human Agent transfer · Phone
> number · Email · Web support · In-store visits · or Support form.*

**Victor answers vendors and couples by automation on every lane.** So this clause binds every
day the estate operates.

**Derived by command at origin, across all of `src/`:** the only escalation-shaped string in the
codebase is **`hello@thedreamwedding.in`, once**. There is **no in-chat human transfer, no
"talk to a person" path, and no support form** on any lane.

**An email address is a listed escalation path**, so the estate is not plainly outside the
clause — but *"prompt, clear, and direct"* is doing work, and a single address buried in one file
is none of those from the user's side. **What is owed is not a feature; it is a sentence a user
can see** — one line in Victor's repertoire that hands over an address or a number when asked,
and the same contact on the WABA business profile per §1.

**Owed to the founder:** what the WABA's business profile actually displays today. Not derivable
from the tree.

## 3 · §3 — THE CONCIERGE LINE, AND IT HOLDS

> *You may not forward or otherwise share information from a customer chat with any other
> customer.*

**The concierge forwards a couple's request form — words she typed to TDW for the purpose of
being matched — not her chat.** That is inside the line, and the chair's reading is right.

**It becomes a standing constraint on D2/D3, and a sharp one:** the brief field must carry
**the couple's own typed words for TDW**, never content Victor or Donna harvested from a chat
thread. **The moment a harvested sentence enters an outsider forward, §3 is breached** — and
harvest exists precisely to lift facts out of threads, so the two arms sit one field apart.

**Also §3, and it matters elsewhere:** *"Don't use any data obtained from us about a person you
message within WhatsApp, **other than the content of message threads**, for any purpose other
than as reasonably necessary to support messaging with that person."* The thread-content
carve-out mirrors Meta Terms §4.1(a). It does **not** relax §4.7(b)'s training prohibition,
which is a separate clause with a separate subject.

## 4 · WHAT THE POLICY ADDS THAT NOBODY NAMED

**§7 — the policy can change without notice.**
> *WhatsApp may update this WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy **without notice** (unless such
> notice is required by law); by continuing to use the WhatsApp Business Services after such
> Change, you consent to such Change.*

**The terms promise notice; this policy does not.** So the document Meta enforces most often is
the one most likely to move silently. **A periodic re-read is the only defence**, and it pairs
naturally with F-41.9's pricing re-check on 09-25 — same sitting, two pages.

**§7 also states the quality mechanism plainly:** people can block or report, and *"our systems
will limit the amount of messages a business can send… if the business' quality tier is low for
a sustained period."* That is the WABA-level restriction **F-41.125 says nothing currently
receives.**

**§4 — one prohibited vertical worth a glance, and it is not TDW.** *Dating services* are
prohibited; a wedding-vendor marketplace is not a dating service and nothing in the estate comes
near it. Recorded so a later reader does not have to wonder.

**§2 — *"Standard pricing applies for all conversation categories."*** Consistent with B4·2's
rate reading; no separate concierge pricing exists to hope for.
