## 4 · WHY IT IS WORDED THIS WAY

- **It names the submission ID in the first line.** Support routes on the ID; a chase that makes
  an agent search for the case is a chase that gets a template answer.
- **It says outright that we are not re-filing and not asking for priority.** Both are the
  things support is braced for, and disclaiming them costs one sentence and buys attention.
- **It offers something.** *Tell me which one and I will supply it the same day* converts a
  chase into an open door. The commonest cause of a stalled review is a reviewer who could not
  reproduce a step and has not yet written to say so.
- **It states the verification fact.** Tech Provider verification on 2026-08-09 is the thing
  that makes the request routine rather than speculative.
- **It asks a closable question.** *Is it queued, or is something needed?* Either answer ends
  the ambiguity; *any update?* does not.
- **It does not mention Embedded Signup, the Instagram permissions, or the second app.** One
  case, one subject. Fusing them invites a single answer that addresses none.
