## 1 · THE HINGE, SETTLED — AND HALF MY EARLIER FLAG WAS WRONG

The annex splits cleanly by which hat Company wears under General Terms §1.4:

- **§2 Processor Terms** — *"To the extent Company is a 'Service Provider' or 'Processor'…"*
- **§3 Controller Terms** — *"To the extent Company is a 'Controller' or **'Third Party'**…"*

**The Tech Provider Terms deem TDW a "Third Party."** So **§3 governs TDW and §2 does not.**

**What that means for the two fragments this seat flagged from the search index:**

| Flagged | Actually |
|---|---|
| Subprocessor approval — notify `subprocessors@meta.com`, 30-day objection window, no appointment until approval or elapse | **§2.1 — Processor-side. DOES NOT BIND TDW.** This was the fragment I said might reach Anthropic, DeepSeek and Z.ai directly. **It does not.** |
| 48-hour incident notice | **§3.6 — inside the Controller Terms. DOES BIND TDW.** |

**One of the two was wrong and I said at the time that fragments read either way.** They did.
Recorded rather than quietly dropped, because the seat that reads fragments next should know the
hit rate was one in two.

**Also not binding, for the same reason:** §2.2's audit-contribution and processing-records
duties, §2.3's duty to challenge an unlawful instruction, §2.4's no-reidentification rule.
Those are Meta's-processor obligations and TDW is not that.

## 2 · WHAT DOES BIND TDW — §3, in full

| § | Obligation | State |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Process Personal Data in accordance with APAC requirements — **including, where applicable, on the basis of the data subject's unambiguous explicit consent** or another valid ground | **India's DPDP Act is the live instrument.** Not assessed by this seat. |
| 3.2 | **Only Process as clearly described in TDW's own privacy policy** or agreement with the data subject | Binds `/privacy` to actual behaviour. A4 just widened that page; **the page is now the boundary of what the estate may do**, not a description of it. |
| 3.3 | Reasonable effort to keep data accurate **if used to make a decision affecting the subject, or disclosed onward** | The concierge match is a decision affecting a couple and a vendor, and the outsider forward is a disclosure onward. |
| 3.4 | **Cease to retain when no longer reasonably necessary** | The estate has no retention schedule. Named. |
| 3.5 | The description of Processing lives in the Agreement | — |
| 3.6 | Incident notice to Meta **without undue delay and in any event within 48 HOURS** | Beside `vendor-incident@meta.com` (R-41.81). **The clock belongs on the dates register.** |

**§3.6 is the sharpest.** The General Terms say *"without undue delay"*; APAC puts a number on
it. **48 hours from discovery**, and a data incident is defined broadly enough in the General
Terms to include loss, unauthorised access, or compromise of any Meta Platforms Data.

## 3 · §4 INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS — and this one is not gated on the hat

**§4 opens** *"To the extent that Company Processes or otherwise transfers Personal Data outside
the jurisdiction in which such data was originally collected…"* — **no Processor/Third-Party
qualifier.** It applies.

- **§4.1** — transfers only per the Agreement, this DPA and Meta's documented instructions.
- **§4.2** — comply with APAC requirements on the transfer, and **ensure the transfer does not
  put Meta Controller in breach.**
- **§4.3** — **"ensure that any transfer of Meta Platforms Data is made in accordance with a
  valid transfer mechanism."**

**THIS IS A NEW OBLIGATION ON A PATH THE ESTATE ALREADY OPERATES.** Message content collected in
India goes to **Anthropic** (US), **DeepSeek** (residency contested — B4·4 could not derive it)
and **Z.ai** (Singapore per its DPA, though B4·5 found no live `glm` route).

**What §4.3 asks for is not a policy line — it is a named mechanism**, and this seat has not
established that any exists for those transfers. It sits beside F-41.48's written-agreement
requirement rather than inside it: §5.a asks *what may the provider do with it*; §4.3 asks
*by what lawful instrument did it cross the border*. **Two different artefacts.**

**Reported, not ruled. This is a question for counsel, not a filing seat** — India's DPDP Act
transfer regime and whichever mechanism the estate relies on are outside what can be derived
from a repository. **Named because it was invisible until the annex was read**, which is the
whole argument for reading annexes.
