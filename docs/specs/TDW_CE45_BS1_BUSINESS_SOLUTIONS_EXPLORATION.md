### §1.4 Platform facts (read 2026-09-24)

**AI Providers clause.** https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/business-solution-terms (Last Modified March 6, 2026). AI providers are barred when AI is "the primary (rather than incidental or ancillary) functionality ... as determined by Meta in its sole discretion". When a business retains an AI provider as a Third Party Service Provider, Business Solution Data (including anonymous, aggregate or derived forms) may not be used to create, develop, train or improve any AI model (fine-tuning for exclusive use excepted). Separately: no use of Business Solution Data to "track, build, or augment profiles on individual WhatsApp users", and no sharing of derived data to third parties. **Consequence:** G7 pricing intelligence must be built from invoices, never from chats. This also settles F-41.33's reading 1: the ban is in the Business Solution Terms, binding since Jan 2026.
