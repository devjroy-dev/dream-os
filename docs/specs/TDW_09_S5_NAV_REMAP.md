## 1 · VENDOR — the fork, two arms, one recommendation

**The disease being cured** (named once): today's bar is **one bar with two memberships** — mode `studio` shows Calendar·Business·More, mode `discover` shows Portfolio·Leads·Collab — so the nav a vendor learns on Monday is not the nav she sees on Tuesday. *A nav whose membership changes is a nav you cannot learn* (recognition-over-recall, applied to the chrome itself). Stage 2 adds: both memberships give zero pressed feedback.

**ARM (a) — FIVE STABLE DOORS, the mode dissolved.** *(recommended)*
`Home · Calendar · Business · Storefront · More` — one membership, forever.
- **Home** — the R-X23 three-zone rest state, with the R-X22 hybrid chat rising from its input. *(thumb-zone: the primary destination is where the app opens; serial-position: first seat.)*
- **Calendar** — inbound-degree 4, and a wedding vendor's clock; second seat. *(frequency + serial-position: high-use near the end… seats ordered by use with the two heaviest at the ends — Home first, More last is convention's own anchor.)*
- **Business** — the ledger slices (leads · clients · invoices · expenses · events), absorbing today's `list` machinery unchanged. *(hub-and-spoke: one door to all money-and-people.)*
- **Storefront** — the ruled word (R-X20) becomes a *place*: Portfolio (degree 6), Discover status, Discover leads, Collab as its sections. *(information scent: the label predicts the destination — everything about how she appears to couples lives behind the word that says so.)*
- **More** — Settings · TDS · Contracts · Couture · Featured · Team Hub · **Notes** (the R-X8 re-home lands here, its ruled seat) · the Advisor chip pinned top per the spec's own P1.
**The mode seam's fate under (a):** the ModePill retires — there is nothing left for it to switch; the VictorModeChip (Business/Advisor) survives as the *one* mode control, in its R-X20 distinct-chip silhouette, and the R-U17 confusability dies by subtraction rather than by dress. **Trade, stated:** Portfolio drops from a first-row tab (in discover-mode) to one tap deeper inside Storefront — paid for by every other destination becoming permanently findable; degree-6 Portfolio gets Storefront's first section as compensation.
**Every current seat accounted** (the CE-115 discipline): Calendar KEPT (seat 2) · Business KEPT (seat 3) · More KEPT (seat 5) · Portfolio MOVED → Storefront §1 (reason above) · Leads MOVED → Storefront §3 *and* surfaced at Home's waiting zone (the daily case never needs the tab) · Collab MOVED → Storefront §4 (degree 3, weekly-not-daily) · the Studio/Discover pill REMOVED-BY-DISSOLUTION (this paper's arm (a), the founder's ruling to make).

**ARM (b) — TWO WINGS.** Work/Storefront as R-X20 top tabs; bottom nav per wing (Work: Home·Calendar·Business·More; Storefront: Profile·Leads·Collab·More). *Honest case for it:* preserves the two-worlds mental model the current product teaches. *Against, and why it is not recommended:* it keeps two navigation systems where the spec's own U-1 ruled **five doors once already** (*"Hub · Ledger · Calendar · Studio · More"* — arm (a) is U-1 with the ruled vocabulary), and it keeps membership-switching, the exact disease.

## 2 · BRIDE — one sun becomes a hub

**The disease:** sanctuary is a 3,900-line absorption — degree 7 against everything-else ≤2 — with its siblings dead or orphaned (IA map §3.2). She has one room and hallways nobody walks.
**THE PROPOSAL — FOUR DOORS:** `Home · Muse · Journey · Circle`.
- **Home** = sanctuary's feed-and-slices heart, *shed of* the journey/circle limbs it absorbed. *(one-purpose-per-screen; the mega-page decomposes along the seams its own slices already draw.)*
- **Muse** — her saved looks (the arm-(a) grid as ruled at R-X18). *(frequency of intent: the save-and-dream loop is the product's bride promise.)*

**Trade, stated:** decomposing sanctuary is the bride lane's largest structural act and rides the same cure phase as Warm Porcelain + F-09.27 (the same lines move); the mock shows the after-shape so the founder rules the *destination* now and the chair sequences the surgery.
