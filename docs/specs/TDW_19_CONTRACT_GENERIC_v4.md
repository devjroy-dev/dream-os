## WHAT THIS FILE IS, AND WHAT v1 AND v3 STILL ARE

`TDW_19_CONTRACT_GENERIC_v1.md` is **the record the lawyer approved** — eighteen clauses, seven annexes. It stays on the tree, untouched.

`TDW_19_CONTRACT_GENERIC_v3.md` is **the instrument the lawyer approved a second time** (R-40.46) and **the record of the walked signature** — the bytes the signature-mechanism walk witnessed. It stays on the tree, untouched. **R-40.46 is superseded as to which instrument a Vendor sends; it is not withdrawn as to what v3 was.**

**This is v4, and from R-40.106 it is what a Vendor sends.** The same agreement, in the register the founder asked for. Formal, short, crisp. Proper but simple legal terminology. The parties are **the Vendor** and **the Client**, defined once. Third person throughout. No contractions. Every clause a numbered heading, with sub-clauses where obligations genuinely separate and not where they do not — that restraint is **R-40.43 governing length**, not a house style applied unevenly.

### What changed from v3, and what did not

**The substance did not change.** Every protection the lawyer passed is here. What changed is the voice, plus two additions and one split he ruled:

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| "we" and "you" → **the Vendor** and **the Client** | The founder's register. The Client is **one party comprising two persons**, defined at 1.2, jointly and severally bound — v3's own sentence survives at 1.3. |
| **Clause 5 · Accommodation and Travel — NEW** | R-40.73. v3 carried travel and stay as one sentence inside Money. It is now its own clause with its own obligations. |
| **Clause 9 · Venue and Location — NEW heading** | v3's clause 7 carried the Client's obligations and the venue's conditions in one list. The venue's conditions are a different subject and now say so. |
| v3 clause 11 "The short ones" → **13, 14, 15** | Liability, confidentiality and data, and governing law are three clauses. "The short ones" is not a heading in the register the founder named. |
| **The annexes are set out in full, each as its own titled page** | F-40.190: the renderer named its annexes and printed none of them, so a couple could not find what was included in her own agreement. The instrument states them; sitting 2 makes the renderer print them. |

### Three rules that carry into every page

**Every number is a field**, written like `{{deposit_pct}}`, filled by the Vendor. **TDW chooses none of them.**

**Money reads `Rs 1,25,000`** — Indian grouping, the letters `Rs`, never the rupee sign, never `K`, `L` or `Cr`.

**Nothing leaves as an underscore — R-40.88.** A clause whose fields are unset is **absent from the document**; it does not print `N/A`, a blank rule, or a greyed zero. A field the act itself requires stops the act at Send. Each clause's class is in the register's Omission column and is stated in the margin note under each clause below.
