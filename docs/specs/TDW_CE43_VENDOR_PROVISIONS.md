## §1 · Part A · Provisions

Each: what happens · why she pays · what it needs · when · Victor.

**P1 · "Someone picks up at 11pm"** (floor 1). An enquiry lands at 11:40pm. Inside a minute the couple hears back in her voice, on her number: whether the date is free, what her usual package looks like, and a question about the venue. She wakes to three quotes already sent and three leads filed. She set the rules once on the PWA: which package to quote, and which dates never to promise. *Pays:* the enquiry she used to lose overnight is booked by breakfast. *Needs:* her own number, either way [M3]; the spine's calendar and packages. Runs on TDW's line before her own number exists, in TDW's voice. *When:* VP-1, after G6. *Victor:* veto slot.

**P2 · "My packages, right in the chat"** (floor 2). Her packages sit as a catalogue inside WhatsApp. A couple taps "Ask about this" and the enquiry arrives with the package already chosen. *Pays:* couples arrive knowing what they want and roughly what it costs. *Needs:* her own number, the moved way; Meta's catalogues cover services [M4]; the View catalog button opens it inside WhatsApp [M5c]. Shared way: see B3. *When:* after G6, moved-way vendors only. *Victor:* none.

**P3 · "One thread for the whole wedding"** (floor 3; **bride lane, after Block 09, R-43.12**). The bride's thread on TDW's line tells her what she owes this month across every vendor, and who arrives when on each day, from the spine. *Pays:* her couples stop calling to ask "when do you come?" *Needs:* the bride line; the spine's data. *Victor:* bride-line copy to the veto slot.

**P4 · "Paid, with one tap"** (floor 4). The invoice reaches the couple with a pay button; the money lands in her own account; she taps "received" and the milestone closes; nothing passes through TDW. *Pays:* no more screenshot-matching at month end. *Needs:* her own number plus her own payment gateway; Meta's India payments use an invoice-style message whose link comes from the business's gateway, with status from that gateway [M8]. A plain UPI link with no gateway is B4. *When:* VP-3, after LC-3 and G6. *Victor:* none.

**P5 · "My own offers, my own name"** (floor 5). She sends her own festive offer from her own number to couples who opted in to her; TDW files the template for her by code; she pays Meta directly; her quality rating is her own. *Pays:* her reach no longer rides on anyone else's reputation. *Needs:* her own number [M3]; template filing by code (IDEAS-1 A10). *When:* after G6. *Victor:* none.

**P6 · "Tap to call me"** (floor 6). The couple taps a call button inside the chat; a missed call becomes a lead with a callback offered and a holding line. *Pays:* no missed call goes unanswered. *Needs:* her own number, moved way only, daily limit 2,000 [M1]; call-button message inside an open window [M2, seen]. *When:* after G6, moved-way vendors only. *Victor:* veto slot.

## §5 · Part E · A photographer's week on TDW (the page to read first)

Meera shoots weddings in Delhi. She connected her number to TDW last month and kept her WhatsApp Business app, so nothing about her phone changed except that her old chats now live on her records too.

On Monday she wakes to three enquiries that came in between eleven and two. Each couple already has an answer: the date is free, here is what her usual package holds, and which venue is it? Two of them came with the date, city and guest count already filled in, because the couples had put those in once on their planning app. She opens her phone to find three new leads and three quotes sent, and she did not type a word. One couple she knows from last winter, and she can see that because her six months of chats came with her.

On Tuesday she answers a couple herself, from her phone, between shoots, and tells them a price. By evening that price sits on their lead as the quote, and the lead has moved forward. She did not open anything else to make that happen.

On Wednesday two couples ask for the same Saturday in February. The first pays the advance in the afternoon. The money lands in her own account, she taps "received", and the date is theirs. The second couple is told kindly that the date is on hold and that they will hear first if it opens. Nobody gets a "sorry, it went" from her.

On Thursday a couple taps her Instagram ad. She replies once, and for the next three days everything she sends them costs her nothing. By Friday they have her package and her calendar, and they book.

Friday is a wedding. At seven in the morning her slice of the day arrives: the gate, the time, and the name of the person on site. She can see the makeup artist on this wedding is also on TDW and finishes at four, so she plans the portraits for half past. Stuck in traffic on the way to the pheras, she tells her assistant she is twenty minutes late, and the couple's thread and the decorator both know before anyone has to call her.

On Saturday a couple from October gets a note that their gallery is halfway done. She never wrote it, and they never had to ask. Another couple, whose album went out last week, is asked how it went and says yes with one button. By evening there is a review on her storefront and her name has gone to a friend of theirs who is getting married in the spring.

On Sunday her brief shows the week in a few lines: how fast she answered, how many enquiries turned into quotes, how many quotes turned into bookings, and what each booking was worth. It also lists two couples from August who asked about dates that have since opened. She taps one of them and leaves the other.

## §6 · ADS-1 · the ads track (R-43.13)

**What the vendor gets.** An "Ads" room on the PWA: she picks a package, a budget and dates; the ad runs as her Page and her Instagram from her own ad account on her own card; the click lands in her WhatsApp on the free three-day window (P12); Victor answers as her front desk (P1); the Sunday brief shows what each rupee of spend became (P8). The spend never touches TDW; zero commission holds.

**What Meta must grant, a separate review from the WhatsApp one.** `ads_management` (create, edit, pause campaigns in her ad account) and `ads_read` (results back into the brief) at Advanced access; `business_management` (list the ad accounts her business owns); the Page and Instagram permissions so the ad runs as her Page and handle (`pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`, `pages_manage_ads`, `instagram_basic`; **this set is unverified; confirm in the App Dashboard before submitting**); the Marketing API access tier (Limited to Full is earned by usage, threshold 500 calls in 15 days); Business Verification confirmed. Review expects a written use case and a screencast of a test user creating or editing a campaign on the platform. Embedded Signup v4 can onboard a vendor to click-to-WhatsApp ads and the Conversions API in the same connect flow [M9], so G6's flow carries the ads grant once approved.

**Sequence.** The founder applies now (no byte in the tree); the seat that charters ADS-1 drafts the use case and the screencast script for him; VP-5 builds on approval, after G6.
