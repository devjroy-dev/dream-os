## 2 · THE CURRENT MACHINERY, DERIVED
**The hood:** `components/vendor/Cabinet.tsx` — a brass **"Your books"** handle (`:326`, `aria-label="Open your books"`) on the Hub lifts a bottom sheet over **six read-only slices**, rendered through **three skins — Cards / Workbench / Accounts** (`type Skin`, `:21`), the skin chosen inside the sheet and persisted at `:277`/`:303`. Read-only by design (its own header: *"it paints what Kriya kept; it never writes"*).
**Two convictions found in the derivation, filed in this stage's ledger:** the skin persists via **`localStorage`** (`SKIN_KEY`, `:277`/`:303`) against the house no-localStorage guardrail — while `VictorModeChip.tsx:7`'s own comment boasts *"persists to the server … (no localStorage)"* two components away (**F-09.25**); and the sheet prints money through **`fmtINR` — the ₹ glyph — at `Cabinet.tsx:63`/`:65`** (`lib/vendor/cabinet.ts` home), a vendor-facing register breach F-09.6's twelve-formatter census missed (**F-09.24**, the thirteenth home).
**The chat and the Hub:** `app/vendor/page.tsx` (595 lines) composes the Ledger strip (three brass cells, `:157`), the ChatThread scroll surface (`:519`), and the Cabinet handle — chat and books already share one screen; the "hood" is a *reveal within* it, not a navigation away.
**"The landing page should disappear…":** for a **logged-in vendor** the landing is not on this path at all — first open lands on the Hub (the IA map §3.1); under the ruled **O-B home** it lands on the one-page home with the assistant as a persistent input bar. The founder's question therefore resolves to: *what happens when the vendor taps that input bar* — and that is exactly what the three models below differ on.

## 3 · THE THREE MODELS (all walkable in the mock, current alongside)

**MODEL 1 — THE RISING ROOM** *(closest to today, one change)*: O-B home; tapping the input **lifts the chat as a full-height sheet over the home** (the Cabinet's own gesture grammar, reused — one physics for both reveals). Books handle stays on the home beneath. Skin switcher moves **into the sheet's header** as three tiny labeled glyphs. *Leans on:* consistency (one reveal grammar), recognition (nothing moves rooms), Fitts (input bar bottom, thumb-native). *Cost:* chat-over-home means the home's glanceable state hides while chatting.
**MODEL 2 — TWO ROOMS, ONE HALL**: the home and the chat are **two full screens**, the input bar on the home is a *door* (tap → navigate; the landing-question's "disappears completely" answered literally). Books becomes a home **section** (the top of the six slices inline, "See all" → `/vendor/list`), the sheet retired; skins retire with it — Workbench/Accounts become the `/vendor/list` slice views they already mirror. *Leans on:* progressive disclosure, one-purpose-per-screen, and it deletes a whole control class (the skin switcher) instead of re-dressing it. *Cost:* the beloved rising-books moment dies; the biggest change.
**MODEL 3 — THE SPLIT HOOD**: chat stays inline on the home (today's shape); the Books handle moves **into the bottom nav's Business tab as a peek-header** (the sheet rises from the Business door, where the books conceptually live), skin switcher rendered as **named tabs inside the sheet** rather than an unlabeled cycle. *Leans on:* information scent (books under Business), recognition (skins named where used). *Cost:* the Hub loses its brass moment; two surfaces now own ledger-shaped UI.

**Recommendation: MODEL 1.** It answers all three founder questions with the smallest byte radius, reuses the estate's one proven reveal physics, and keeps the O-B ruling intact; Model 2 is the right *second* step if the skins prove unused in live telemetry, and the paper says so rather than pretending the fork is final. Every skin/label word rides the veto.
